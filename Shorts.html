Zenitsu Vs Spider Demon

Inside Mount Natagumo, Zenitsu Agatsuma was terrified and poisoned by a spider demon. Even while shaking in fear, he kept moving through the dark forest. When the demon attacked, Zenitsu suddenly entered his sleep state and unleashed Thunder Breathing First Form. In a single lightning-fast strike, golden thunder flashed through the forest and the spider demon was defeated instantly. The moment became one of the most iconic scenes in Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba because it showed Zenitsu’s hidden power behind his fear. ⚡

Tanjiro Vs Rui

During the battle at Mount Natagumo, Tanjiro Kamado faced Rui, one of the strongest spider demons. Rui’s deadly threads easily cut through trees and trapped Demon Slayers. As the fight became desperate, Tanjiro remembered his father’s dance and combined it with Water Breathing, creating a powerful new attack. With flames surrounding his blade, Tanjiro charged forward while protecting Nezuko. At the same moment, Nezuko used her Blood Demon Art to ignite Rui’s threads. The fiery attack became one of the most legendary moments in Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba, showing the strength of the Kamado siblings’ bond. 🔥

Rengoku Vs Akaza

After the Mugen Train mission, Kyojuro Rengoku faced the Upper Rank demon Akaza in a brutal battle under the night sky. Akaza’s overwhelming strength pushed the Flame Hashira to his limits, but Rengoku refused to step back. With blazing sword strikes and fearless determination, he protected everyone behind him. Even after suffering fatal injuries, Rengoku continued fighting and nearly trapped Akaza until sunrise. The battle became one of the most emotional moments in Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba, proving why Rengoku earned the respect of every Demon Slayer. 🔥

Tengen Vs Gautaro

Inside the Entertainment District, Tengen Uzui battled the deadly Upper Rank demon Gyutaro in one of the fastest fights in the series. Explosions shook the city as Tengen used his Sound Breathing techniques against Gyutaro’s poisonous blood attacks. Even after being heavily injured, Tengen kept fighting alongside Tanjiro. The battle reached its peak with flashing blades, massive explosions, and nonstop action across the rooftops. This fight became one of the most visually intense moments in Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba thanks to its animation, speed, and energy. 💥

Mitsuri Vs Hantengu

During the Swordsmith Village battle, Mitsuri Kanroji faced Hantengu’s powerful demon forms while protecting the entire village. Using her flexible Love Breathing sword, Mitsuri moved with incredible speed and agility, cutting through attacks while keeping the demons away from the villagers. Even when overwhelmed by the enemy’s strength, Mitsuri continued fighting with a smile and refused to give up. The battle showed why Mitsuri is one of the strongest Hashira in Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba, combining beauty, speed, and overwhelming power. 🌸

Sanemi and Obanai in Action

During the Hashira Training Arc, Sanemi Shinazugawa and Obanai Iguro pushed Demon Slayers through brutal combat training to prepare for the coming war. Their intense training was suddenly interrupted by a demon attack, forcing both Hashira into action. Sanemi’s aggressive Wind Breathing and Obanai’s precise Serpent Breathing overwhelmed the enemies with incredible speed. The moment highlighted the terrifying strength of the Hashira in Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba and showed how dangerous the final battles would become. 🌪️🐍Last updated 3 mins ago
