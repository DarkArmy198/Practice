Giyu Tomioka

Giyu Tomioka is the Water Hashira of the Demon Slayer Corps. He is calm, quiet, and serious during battles. Giyu uses powerful Water Breathing techniques against demons. He is one of the strongest swordsmen in the Hashira.

Kyojuro Rengoku

Kyojuro Rengoku Kyojuro Rengoku is the Flame Hashira of the Demon Slayer Corps. He is known for his courage, passion, and positive attitude. Rengoku uses powerful Flame Breathing techniques in combat. He is admired for his strong spirit and determination.

Shinobu Kocho

Shinobu Kocho is the Insect Hashira of the Demon Slayer Corps. She is intelligent, calm, and always appears cheerful. Shinobu uses poison-based attacks to defeat demons. Her speed and agility make her a dangerous fighter.

Tengen Uzui

Tengen Uzui is the flashy Sound Hashira of the Corps. He fights using explosive attacks and dual Nichirin swords. Tengen has a loud personality and loves flashy things. He is highly skilled and extremely fast in battle.

Mitsuri Kanroji

Mitsuri Kanroji is the cheerful Love Hashira of the Corps. She is kind-hearted, emotional, and incredibly strong. Mitsuri uses unique Love Breathing sword techniques. Her flexibility and speed make her powerful in combat.

Muichiro Tokito

Muichiro Tokito is the talented Mist Hashira of the Corps. He is calm, quiet, and often lost in thought. Muichiro uses fast and unpredictable Mist Breathing attacks. Despite his age, he is an extremely skilled swordsman.

Obanai Iguro

Obanai Iguro is the Serpent Hashira of the Demon Slayer Corps. He fights alongside his snake companion named Kaburamaru. Obanai uses sharp and twisting Serpent Breathing attacks. He is strict, serious, and highly dedicated to duty.

Sanemi Shinazugawa

Sanemi Shinazugawa is the aggressive Wind Hashira of the Corps. He is known for his anger and fearless personality. Sanemi uses destructive Wind Breathing techniques in battle. He is one of the strongest and toughest Hashira.

Gyomei Himejima

Gyomei Himejima is the Stone Hashira of the Demon Slayer Corps. He is calm, wise, and deeply respected by others. Gyomei uses immense strength with Stone Breathing techniques. He is considered the strongest among all the Hashira.

Master Kagaya Ubuyashiki

Kagaya Ubuyashiki is the leader of the Demon Slayer Corps. He is calm, wise, and deeply respected by all the Hashira. People also call him Oyakata-sama with great respect. His guidance and kindness inspire every demon slayer.
